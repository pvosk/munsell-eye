# Banked backward setup-region refinement — 12 September 2026

## Scope and result

Six archived fixed destinations received deeper backward endpoint-region expansion, plus two explicitly new neighboring destinations in the warm-heavy palette with Cobalt Teal. The original six target colors were not recentered. The two neighbors alter the known target recipe's teal share by up to .12, producing fixed new goals before backward analysis; these are deliberate reachable neighbors, not an unbiased target sample.

The pass samples 845 acceptable endpoint recipes and constructs 27,992 legal backward routes, retaining 198 representatives across starting base, finishing paint, value movement, non-value-only finish travel, inward movement, and addition count. Every previously archived raw/efficient competitor for the original goals is retained in the challenger input. The stronger recipe-blind search considers every base/order up to three additions, with 288 samples, twelve restarts, 180 iterations and the original 55 ms / .04 timing/setup support policy. This is still bounded numerical evidence, not a proof of global minima.

The important outcome is **repeatable warm-family setup → Cobalt Teal finish availability, not elimination of alternative starting strategies**. None of these eight cases becomes a generally eligible majority-base ride or value-shift under the existing checks. They remain useful one-off/style opportunities and archived refinement evidence.

## Warm-heavy palette, one contrasting cool paint

Paints: Cadmium Lemon / Naples Yellow Hue / Transparent Orange / Cadmium Red Light / Cobalt Teal. The original target is approximately RGB (87,80,70), a warm muted dark. Exact floating-point RGB is retained in the archive.

| Start | Shortest-found supported additions | Observed opportunity |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cadmium Lemon | 2 | Meaningful setup and final glide into Cobalt Teal |
| Naples Yellow Hue | 2 | Meaningful setup and final glide into Cobalt Teal |
| Transparent Orange | 1 | Simpler direct alternative |
| Cadmium Red Light | 1 | A chromatic ride into teal is available, without the same setup structure |
| Cobalt Teal | 1 | Simpler reverse-direction approach remains |

The original target has ride availability from three of five bases, glide availability from two, and no recorded general failure flags. Supported non-ride alternatives are present from every base. This is not a forced cross-family route.

The selected Naples → Cadmium Red Light → Cobalt Teal setup/finish slice has at least one successful finish in 22 of 33 sampled setup rows. It is therefore not only an isolated matching recipe. This count does **not** mean 22 rows all have 55 ms finishing windows, is not a probability over all possible setups, and does not establish equal difficulty across starting bases. The full accepted dose samples and exact witness are saved.

## Numerical limits and archive

Endpoint expansion uses 32 random recipe directions plus omit-one directions, first connected acceptance segments, and three locations along each ray. Seven finishing-share fractions are peeled backward. Prefixes are exact recipe orders requiring no more than two additions before the finish. Higher-dimensional setups, disconnected regions and longer prefixes can be missed. The legacy endpoint helper's literal default type was locally widened without changing its runtime implementation or previous source files.

- [Exact targets, endpoint/predecessor witnesses, competitors, and setup slices](play-banked-backward-refinement.json).
- Runner: `scripts/refine-banked-setups.ts`; tests: `scripts/test-banked-refinement.ts`.
- Original broad bank remains intact. No strengths, mass, timing, tolerance, game presentation, par, or playable lab specimens changed.
- Replay/fingerprint tests and type checking passed. The final deterministic run took about 99 seconds locally. A preliminary identical-runtime run is retained only in ignored outputs after a TypeScript annotation correction.

Next design interpretation: keep the warm cluster plus one contrasting cool paint as an interesting palette identity, without representing its current holes as universally protected. The new evidence supports that identity more strongly than it supports adding another rigid style label.
