# Journey inspection — frozen protocol

Scope: offline engine and deeper palette/hole inspection. The public v55 lab, historical banks, starting mixture rules, paint model, landing tolerance, charge mechanics, par and animations remain unchanged.

## Four separate layers

1. **General playability:** every base needs a timing-supported meaningful route. A base already inside the scoring cup fails. Supported short direct one-pour shortcuts and efficient token-pour bypasses fail. Unlike the old evaluator, being within 1.8 tolerances is not itself a rejection: close starts can be indirectly demanding. No scoring threshold is changed.
2. **Competing approaches:** retain dose/order variants with different traits and directness, including longer routes, rather than a single featured route. Report additions, timing support, display-world travel/excess, and scoring-space excursion separately. A supported one-pour route shorter than the existing 14-unit floor with less than half a tolerance of excursion is the provisional short-direct diagnostic. A close start (within three tolerances) is called close-indirect only if every retained supported route has at least six excess world units or half a tolerance of excursion. This is evidence within a sampled search, not mathematical necessity. Narrow unsupported routes remain visible through raw route measurements.
3. **Style:** demanding interiors still require three meaningful additions and a 1.1-tolerance two-pour miss margin from every base. Rides, shifts and balancing can qualify with style opportunities from at least two thirds of bases (3/4 or 2/3); resistance is separately reported, not required. Value-shift episodes exclude the free base and first addition: they need meaningful setup and a substantial toward-target value movement, with subsequent cleanup at most 35% of that stroke's travel. A dominant finishing movement supplies at least 25% of total travel. These episode cutoffs are provisional, not validated aesthetic truths. Upward and downward are symmetric. Balancing records both existing opposing-color motion and target-relative value/hue coupling, including lateral deviations that need later correction even if absolute hue/chroma error initially decreases.
4. **Course variety:** require both separated target colors and separated easiest route shapes across shared starting bases. Use nine arc-length-spaced Oklab path samples. A conservative greedy subset rejects a pair if targets are within two scoring tolerances OR any shared base has retained efficient routes with average path-sample distance below 0.05 Oklab units. This is not an optimal packing or a validated sameness classifier. Keep all qualifying individual targets in a separate archive, even if excluded from a palette portfolio.

All existing 55 ms finishing-window, 4% local setup-support, 36-unit ride and 80% chromatic-fraction criteria are retained. New path/episode criteria refine measurements; they do not relax the game. Profiles remain explainable, not one weighted fun score.

## Search plan, fixed before execution

- Pool: 85 catalogue paints plus modeled Flake White and Chartreuse. RGBs and strengths are not optimized.
- Eight untouched controls: UltraOx Dual, Zorny, RYB, CMY, Secondaries, Cobalt Ember, Crimson Current, Orange Echo.
- Twenty-four independently constructed palettes: four each from random, secondary, value-span, chromatic-triad, opposed-pair, and dark-plus-light families. These are independent constructions, not mutations of player favorites. The families are intentionally stratified, not a uniform sample of all combinations. Within-family paint selection is seeded random; categories describe proposal families, never qualify a hole.
- Every palette: 24 broad targets distributed across full mixtures, pairs, triples and near-base mixtures. Add two interior gap-selected targets from 96 proposals, then four competition-directed target mutations each for ride, value shift and balance (38 audited targets per palette).
- Rank training portfolios and specialist opportunities. Freeze eight palette finalists before using the fresh target seed; include Secondaries and Cobalt Ember controls. Evaluate 36 fresh targets per selected palette with no adaptive target tuning on that cohort.
- Densely verify up to three candidates per style per selected palette. Recheck one finalist per selected palette at 513/97×97 resolution, adding numerical three-dose multistart search across all orders (including repeats). Stronger checks override weaker claims and failed targets remain in the inspection record.
- Maintain distinct target, route, value-zone and chroma-zone summaries; do not sum overlapping style columns as independent holes.

No player review recipe is injected into the discovery cohort. The latest review informs the measurement design, so these are calibrated hypotheses, not independent evidence of enjoyment. The three-dose search remains bounded and can miss routes. Finite samples cannot prove a shortcut absent. Palette quality is additionally limited by the game's RGB-derived spectral approximation and modeled strengths.

Implementation: `app/play-journey-analysis.ts`; runner: `scripts/inspect-journey-palettes.ts`; numerical and preservation tests: `scripts/test-play-journeys.ts`. Source fingerprints and exact paints/recipes/witnesses accompany the results. Raw player notes are not copied into the repository.
