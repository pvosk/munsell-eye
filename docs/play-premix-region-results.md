All new holes retain pigment strengths, normalized mass, .0294 landing tolerance
and experimental par 3. No campaign, free-base, sound or charge-bar changes.
Scene difficulty stage is held at zero for this collection so its position in the
11-hole list does not progressively shrink the mixture character. Collection
progress is displayed independently. Historical A/B snapshots remain unchanged.

## Actual computation

Two proposal runs across 13 existing palettes, including three-, four- and
five-paint sets. No pigments were substituted or invented.

- 12,480 target recipe anchors.
- 124,800 accepted ending-recipe samples, including target centers and noncentral
  accepted colors. These samples are not necessarily unique.
- 249,600 backward route draws; 107,668 legal draws before meaning/style gates.
- 168 style-ranked candidate challenges; 45 passed the proposal support and
  no-shorter-route gates. Counts include overlapping-family proposals.
- Nine distinct finalists received a third, stronger numerical challenge: 512
  samples/order, 16 local restarts, through their full featured depth, plus
  accepted-control perturbations. 1,695,782 additional endpoint evaluations.
- Search computation took about 27 seconds for run 1 and 83 seconds for run 2;
  proposal counts are not claims of equally deep validation of every draw.

Final selection capped repeated palettes, avoided near-duplicate destinations
within a palette, retained family coverage, and kept the two surviving three-shot
assemblies. This is a curated measurement lab, not a calibrated campaign.

## What changed our conclusions

Two coupled-balance holes looked protected when considering center-optimized
solutions. Perturbing accepted release controls revealed equally short,
timing-supported paths that fall outside the operational balancing definition.
They are explicitly **competitive**, not required. The rival review includes a
representative bypass instead of showing only the attractive planned line.

The old first-shot Zorn rise and easy Secondaries played attempts do not satisfy
the new whole-route labels. However the pleasant Crimson Current actual attempt
also fails those whole-route tests because of corrections/repetitions. That is a
retained limitation: these classifiers do not predict segment-level pleasure.

Neither a universal near-base rule nor a tighter cup was used. Zorny's three-shot
candidate starts about 2.62 tolerances away, closer than many others, yet survived
one/two-shot searches. Its relevance is structural, not merely distance.

## What remains unproven

This is accepted-region sampling plus backward proposals and local setup scans,
not optimization over the entire inverse image. No global minimum proof, full
recovery-tree analysis, perceptual enjoyment model, or broad palette superiority
claim is made. These new holes have not been human playtested yet. The public lab
is where the family descriptors and candidate quality will be tested together.

See `play-premix-region-design.md`, `play-premix-evidence-1.json`, both immutable
`play-premix-regions-{1,2}.json` runs, and `play-premix-regions-final.json` for exact
recipes, controls, seeds, counters and replayable disagreements.
