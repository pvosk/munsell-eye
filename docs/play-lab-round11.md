# Round 11 — harder setups

Responds to Round 10 feedback: breadth was useful, but 28/30 candidates offered a found one-addition solution from at least one base. This set changes selection, not pigment strength, charging, tolerance or scoring geometry.

## Eight holes

- Secondaries: two archived gap-proposal targets, freshly rechecked; three additions found from every base.
- Zorny: one freshly rechecked gap-proposal target; three additions found from every base.
- Cobalt Ember: one freshly rechecked gap-proposal target; three additions found from every base.
- Lemon Violet Blue (Titanium White, Cadmium Lemon, Dioxazine Purple, Phthalo Blue Green Shade): fixed warm-cream and lavender inverse-planning destinations. Cream: 2/2/2/3 additions by base; lavender: 3/3/3/3.
- Umber Quartet and Ochre/Maroon/Cerulean: one explicitly lighter contrast each, repeated from Round 10. One-addition approaches remain and are disclosed.

The five three-addition targets agree in raw and timing-supported minima across all bases. The cream target also agrees between those measurements. These are shortest FOUND routes in bounded searches, not mathematical proofs or guaranteed subjective difficulty. References may have appeared in prior research; this is not marketed as eight newly generated targets.

## Validation and packaging

Every selected target was rechecked through backward-verification.verify with archived witnesses plus an independent search (up to three additions, 288 samples, 12 restarts, 180 iterations). Six core holes require no general failures and found raw/supported minima of at least two from every base. All eight passed. Full results are in play-hard-lab-selection.json.

Fixed RGB inverse goals stay fixed: the runtime constructs target colors directly from archived RGB rather than recentering on an approximate fitted mixture recipe. Tests replay all displayed routes against the actual runtime target, validate saved attempts, transitions and picker availability, and retain all historical lab compatibility.

New palette definitions are lab-only and append after index 45. Round 10 and earlier attempts/IDs are unchanged. Existing playerPar formula is retained. Free base choice, replay, skip and route comparison remain available. No sound changes.

User standing release preference: publish releases to both the existing public Sites URL and https://github.com/pvosk/munsell-eye.git unless instructed otherwise.
